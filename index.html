Key Assumptions & Methodology

  • Salary Growth: The calculator assumes your salary—and therefore your dollar contribution amounts—will grow annually at the same rate as inflation. This keeps your savings effort constant as a % of income.
  • Retirement Withdrawals: Your desired retirement income and Social Security estimate are inflated into the future. The portfolio only needs to cover the gap between the two.
  • Nominal Values: The table displays nominal values (future dollars), i.e., the amounts you would actually see in your accounts in those years.
  • Employer Contributions: Your and your spouse's employer contributions are treated as part of the annual savings, reducing what you personally need to contribute.
  • Portfolio Return: Expected annual return is calculated as a weighted average of the broad asset classes you select in the allocation panel.
